Protein Backstory

Where your protein came from, and who it's owed to.

Every protein in a public database came from a living thing, a place and, often, people who knew it long before science did. The database rarely tells you. Protein Backstory reads what the records do say and turns it into a card you can link, cite and pin to your work, with nothing to fill in.

The paper Protein Backstory: Provenance Notes for Biodesigners doi:10.5281/zenodo.24441516

03 · Many proteins

    
Make a ledger

    
For a project, a studio library or a company portfolio. Paste UniProt IDs (any format, one or many per line), or drop a text, CSV or FASTA file. You get one spreadsheet, filled in from the records, with a link to each protein's card. Up to 100 proteins at a time.

    
    
    

      
      
    

    

    

      

      

      

      
Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Keep it with the project; re-run it later to see what has changed.
