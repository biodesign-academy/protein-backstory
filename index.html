Protein Backstory

Where your protein came from, and who it's owed to.

Every protein in a public database came from a living thing, a place and, often, people who knew it long before science did. The database rarely tells you. Protein Backstory gathers what the records do say, shows what is missing, and leaves room for what only you can add.

  1. 01Paste an IDEach protein in UniProt has a short ID, such as Q6QZR3.
  2. 02Read the recordWe fetch what the public databases say, and list what they leave out.
